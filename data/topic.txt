Iran War Summary (April 2026)

What started the war: The conflict began on Feb 28, 2026, when the U.S.
and Israel launched large-scale airstrikes on Iran, targeting nuclear
and military sites. Iran responded with missile and drone attacks across
the Middle East.

Current military situation: The war has expanded into a regional
conflict involving Iran, the U.S., Israel, and spillover into
neighboring countries. Iran continues missile and drone strikes, while
the U.S. and Israel carry out airstrikes and counter-operations.

Humanitarian impact: The war has caused a major humanitarian crisis,
with over a million people displaced and significant civilian
casualties. Infrastructure such as schools and hospitals has been
heavily damaged.

Global economic impact: The conflict is disrupting global energy
supplies, particularly through the Strait of Hormuz. This has led to
rising oil prices, inflation, and supply chain disruptions worldwide.

Wider effects: Cyberwarfare, airspace closures, evacuations, and global
protests are ongoing as part of the broader impact of the conflict.

Big picture: The situation has escalated into a multi-country regional
war with global consequences, affecting military stability, humanitarian
conditions, and the global economy.
